# 04 · Communication Plan

In a multi-site rollout, the same change affects people who work very differently: executives in board meetings, area managers on the road, and site teams on shift. Each group gets the message in the form that suits them.

| Audience | What they need to know | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|----------|------------------------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Steering committee (COO, FD, Heads of) | Progress, budget, top risks, decisions needed | Steering pack + meeting | Fortnightly | Project Manager |
| Head office teams | Rollout dates, new dashboards, how reports change | Email update + Teams channel | Weekly | Project Manager |
| Area managers | Which of their sites go live and when; their role on the day | Briefing call + site schedule | Before each wave | Head of Operations |
| Site general managers | What happens on go-live day; training slot; who to call | Go-live pack + 1-hour training | 2 weeks and 2 days before go-live | Area Manager |
| Front-of-house teams | Nothing changes at the till; who to tell if something looks wrong | Shift briefing (5 minutes) | Go-live day | Site GM |
| Vendors (POS, platform) | Wave schedule, credential requests, defects | Weekly call + shared tracker | Weekly | Project Manager |

## Key Messages
1. **Why:** faster, more accurate reporting with less manual work for site teams.
2. **What changes:** site managers stop exporting data; dashboards update daily.
3. **What doesn't change:** the way staff use the till and serve guests.
4. **Where to get help:** hypercare hotline and Teams channel, staffed during go-live week.

## Escalation Path
```
Site GM  →  Area Manager  →  Project Manager  →  Head of Operations  →  COO (Sponsor)
```
Anything that affects trading is escalated **immediately** to the Project Manager and Area Manager, without waiting for the next scheduled update.

## Feedback Loop
- 2-question pulse survey after each site's first week.
- Results reviewed at each wave retrospective, and the go-live pack is updated before the next wave.
