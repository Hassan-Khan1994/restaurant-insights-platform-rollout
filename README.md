# 🍽️ Restaurant Insights Platform Rollout — Multi-Site Project Management Case Study

A project management case study for rolling out a **guest and sales insights platform** across **60 restaurants** of a UK restaurant group (*"Harbour & Co. Restaurant Group"*, a fictional company), in **24 weeks**, using a pilot followed by three rollout waves.

> **Author:** Hassan Khan · Project Manager
> **Method:** Hybrid (stage gates and pilot, then wave-based rollout)
> **Tools:** Jira · MS Project · Power BI · Microsoft Teams

---

## 🎯 Project Context
Harbour & Co. runs 60 restaurants. Each site exports POS and guest-feedback data by hand to head office, so weekly reports are late and inconsistent. This project connects every site's POS to a central insights platform and gives operations, finance and marketing a shared, near-real-time view of sales, covers and guest feedback.

## 💡 What Makes Multi-Site Rollouts Different
- **Repeatability:** the same go-live steps run 60 times, so a strong checklist matters more than heroics.
- **Pilot first:** three varied sites prove the process before the wider rollout.
- **Operational timing:** go-lives avoid weekends, bank holidays and peak trading.
- **Communication at scale:** messages must reach head office, area managers and front-of-house teams in the right form.
