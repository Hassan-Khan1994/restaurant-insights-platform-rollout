# 01 · Project Charter

**Project:** Restaurant Insights Platform Rollout
**Organisation:** Harbour & Co. Restaurant Group (fictional)
**Project Manager:** Hassan Khan
**Sponsor:** Chief Operating Officer

---

## 1. Purpose
Replace manual weekly reporting from 60 restaurants with a central insights platform that collects POS and guest-feedback data automatically and presents it in shared dashboards.

## 2. Objectives

| # | Objective | Measure |
|---|-----------|---------|
| O1 | Connect all 60 sites to the platform | 60/60 sites live by week 24 |
| O2 | Remove manual data exports | Zero manual weekly uploads after go-live |
| O3 | Faster reporting | Daily data available by 9am (was weekly, 3 days late) |
| O4 | Adoption by managers | 90% of area and site managers log in weekly |
| O5 | No disruption to trading | Zero POS outages caused by the rollout |

## 3. Scope

**In scope:** POS integration for all sites, guest-feedback feed, data platform setup, standard dashboards (sales, covers, average spend, feedback), training for head office and site managers, hypercare.

**Out of scope:** replacing the POS system, kitchen management systems, payroll and rota tools, bespoke dashboards beyond the standard set.
