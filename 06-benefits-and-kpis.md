# 06 · Benefits & KPIs

Benefits are tracked after go-live, because a rollout is only successful if the business actually uses the platform.

## Benefits Register

| Benefit | KPI | Baseline | Target | Owner | When measured |
|---------|-----|----------|--------|-------|---------------|
| Faster reporting | Time until daily data is available | Weekly, ~3 days late | Daily by 9am | Head of Operations | Month 1 after handover |
| Less manual work | Site manager hours spent on reporting | ~2 hours/week per site | 0 | Head of Operations | Month 1 |
| Trusted data | Match vs. POS end-of-day report | Not measured | ≥ 99.5% | Data Lead | Weekly |
| Adoption | Managers logging in weekly | 0% | ≥ 90% | Head of Operations | Month 3 |
| Better decisions | Actions raised from dashboard insights at area meetings | Not tracked | Recorded monthly | Area Managers | Month 3 |

## Leading vs. Lagging Indicators
- **Leading (during rollout):** sites live vs. plan, sync success rate, training completion.
- **Lagging (after rollout):** adoption, reporting time saved, actions taken from insights.

## Handover
At week 24, the benefits register passes from the project team to the **Head of Operations**, who reports progress to the steering committee for the following three months. That way benefits keep being tracked after the project team moves on.
